# Oath Rules Summary: Campaigns, Cards & Special Mechanics

Reference: Law of Oath (sections cited as §X.Y).

## 1. Campaign (§5.5) — Cost: 2 Supply

### 1.1 Step 1: Choose Defender (§5.5.1)

- Target any **one** other player who rules your site OR whose pawn is at your site
- If no player rules your site, you can target the **bandits** (neutral defender at empty sites)
- Special: Citizen attacking Chancellor or vice versa — that Citizen is NOT Imperial during this campaign

### 1.2 Step 2: Declare Targets & Collect Dice (§5.5.2)

You must declare at least one target. Available targets of the defender:
- **Any sites they rule** (anywhere on the map!) — each adds defense dice shown on site
- **Any relics/banners they hold** (only if their pawn is at your site) — each adds its defense dice
- **Their pawn and favor** (only if their pawn is at your site) — adds dice shown on their board shield
- If defender rules your site, you **must** target your site

**Attack dice**: attacker collects dice equal to warbands on their board (their "force")
**Defense dice**: collected per target as described above

### 1.3 Step 3: Use Battle Plans (§5.5.3)

- Attacker may use any battle plans (§7.5) they rule, once each
- Then defender may use any battle plans they rule, once each
- Bandits use all battle plans they rule at no cost
- **Imperial defense**: if an Imperial player defends, Chancellor joins as Ally. Any Citizen (except attacker if Citizen) may also join as Ally. Allies can use battle plans at Imperial sites or in their own advisers. A specific battle plan pays its cost once and cannot be used by multiple players
- If attack pool would lose a die below zero, add a defense die instead

### 1.4 Step 4: Roll Defense (§5.5.4)

Defense total = sum of:
- **Shields** (single shield icon) on defense dice
- **Double shields** (special face) = doubles total shields rolled so far (stacks: x4, x8, etc.)
- **Warbands at targeted sites** (+ bandits if attacking bandits)
- **Warbands on defender's board** if their pawn is at your site or any targeted site
- Imperial allies add their board warbands if pawn is at attacker's site or any targeted site

### 1.5 Step 5: Roll Attack (§5.5.5)

- Each **skull** rolled immediately kills 1 warband from the attacker's own force (board warbands)
- Attack total = **swords** rolled. Two hollow swords = 1 sword. A single remaining hollow sword = 0

### 1.6 Step 6: Sacrifice

- If attack < defense, attacker may **sacrifice** warbands from their force (board), +1 attack each
- Must sacrifice exactly enough to be victorious (attack > defense)
- Sacrificed warbands are killed

### 1.7 Step 7: Determine Victor

- Attack > Defense → **attacker victorious**
- Attack ≤ Defense → **defender victorious** (ties go to defender)

### 1.8 Step 8: Resolve Defeat (§5.5.6)

The defeated player kills half (rounded down) of warbands in their **force**:
- Attacker's force = warbands on their board
- Defender's force = warbands that added to defense
- Remaining force warbands: attacker moves to board, defender keeps at defense sites
- Cannot hide warbands — bandits just go to their sites
- Imperial losses: Chancellor chooses which warbands in the defending force are killed

### 1.9 Step 9: Resolve Attacker Victory (§5.5.7)

If attacker wins, in order:
1. **Take Rule of Targeted Sites**: place any number of warbands (even 0) from your force onto targeted sites. Citizens can place on Imperial sites. Imperial warbands at captured sites go to Chancellor's board
2. **Take All Targeted Relics and Banners**: take them, burn 2 favor/secrets from banners, flip People's Favor if seized (§2.5.3)
3. **Banish Pawn and Burn Favor**: if you targeted their pawn, make them travel to a site of your choice (no supply cost), burn half their favor (rounded down). Shrouded Wood site power takes precedence

### 1.10 Step 10: Resolve Battle Plans (§5.5.8)

- Victor resolves "If you're victorious" battle plan effects
- Then either player resolves "At end, discard [card]" effects

## 2. Card Types

### 2.1 Denizen Cards (§2.6)

- Have a **suit** (top-left): Arcane, Beast, Discord, Hearth, Nomad, Order
- May have a **restriction banner** (§7.2): tree (site/adviser only) or person (adviser only) or chain (locked — cannot be discarded/moved/swapped once placed)
- Have a **power** at bottom (§7)
- Live at sites or as player advisers
- Can hold favor or secrets (but not both, and not if already occupied)

### 2.2 Edifice Cards (§2.9)

- Double-sided: intact side has suit + restriction banner + power. Ruined side has no suit, cannot Muster or Trade
- Both sides have a power
- Replace denizens in the Chronicle (§8.3.1)
- Built/repaired by Chancellor or Citizens at sites they rule with no edifice

### 2.3 Relic Cards (§2.4)

- Drawn facedown from relic deck when sites are revealed
- **Defense dice**: shown in top-right corner (added when targeted in campaign)
- **Recover cost**: shown on the site card
- Held faceup on player's board after recovery
- Grand Scepter: special relic Chancellor always starts with. Enables citizenship offers and exiling
- Reliquary relics: held on Chancellor's Imperial Reliquary. Cannot be taken by Campaign/Recover. Their powers cannot be used. Do not count toward victory goals

### 2.4 Vision Cards (§2.7)

- 5 Visions total: Conquest (221), Faith (222), Rebellion (223), Sanctuary (224), Conspiracy (225)
- Drawn from world deck during Search — immediately stop drawing, advance Visions Drawn track
- **Playing Visions**:
  - Exiles: play faceup to Revealed Vision space (not an adviser!). If you already have a revealed Vision, discard the old one
  - Chancellor/Citizens: can only play Visions as facedown advisers (except Conspiracy)
  - Anyone can play the Conspiracy faceup (§5.1.4.IV)
- **Conspiracy special**: when played faceup, burn 1 secret to take 1 relic/banner from a player at your site, if you have ≥2 advisers matching their suit. Then return Conspiracy to box, burn 2 favor/secrets from taken banner, flip People's Favor if seized

## 3. Card Powers (§7)

### 3.1 Access (§7.1.1)

You can use a card's power if:
- Your pawn is at the card's site, OR
- You rule the card (it's at a site you rule, or it's your adviser)
- Sites describe how their powers are used

### 3.2 Cost (§7.1.2)

- Powers may cost favor or secrets (place on the card or burn to shared bank)
- Cannot place favor/secrets on a card that already has some
- Facedown secrets cannot be placed or burned to pay costs

### 3.3 Power Types (§7.3)

| Type | When Used | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Wake** (§7.3.1) | During Wake Phase | Mandatory except for favor/secret taking (§4.1.4) |
| **Action** (§7.3.2) | During Act Phase | Minor action, no supply cost |
| **When Played** (§7.3.3) | On playing the card faceup | Gain favor, resolve Homeland site powers, resolve modifiers first |
| **Rest** (§7.3.4) | During Rest Phase | Once each, any order |
| **Persistent** (§7.1.4) | Continuous | Thick braided box. Affects all players the card describes. Takes effect during the action it modifies |

### 3.4 Major Action Modifiers (§7.4)

- Used at the start of the major action they modify (shown by icon in power box)
- **Usually optional** (§7.4.1): choose to use unless "must" or "cannot"
- **Single use** (§7.4.2): modifies current major action only, once per action
- **No discarding active modifiers** (§7.4.3): cannot discard a modifier during the action it's modifying

### 3.5 Battle Plans (§7.5)

- Campaign modifiers with crown icon
- Red = attacker only, Blue = defender only, Gradient = either
- **Added Use Limit** (§7.5.1): can only use at your pawn's site or sites you rule (but not your opponent's)
- **Different Timing** (§7.5.2): used during Campaign step 3 (Use Battle Plans), not at action start
- **No Pay Limit** (§7.5.3): can pay cost even if card has favor/secrets (still only use once)
- **Iconography** (§7.5.4): +/- attack dice or defense dice

## 4. Banners (§2.5)

### 4.1 People's Favor

- Has a variable number of favor tokens on it (starts with 1)
- **Defense dice**: equal to number of favor on it
- **Seize penalty** (§2.5.3): when seized (taken via Recover or Campaign), burns 2 favor (min 1), flips to Mob side if not already
- **Mob side**: People's Favor flips to Mob when it has 6+ favor. Mob side doubles the Wake power (place/return favor twice). Flips back when favor drops below 6
- **Wake power**: holder places or returns 1 favor to/from bank with least favor
- **Holding benefit**: holder of People's Favor can discard a card from any site in their region when playing a card to a full site. Also lets you play cards from any site in your region

### 4.2 Darkest Secret

- Has a variable number of secret tokens (starts with 1)
- **Defense dice**: equal to number of secrets on it
- **Seize penalty**: burns 2 secrets (min 1)
- **Holding benefit**: holder of Darkest Secret draws from world deck at reduced cost / gains search advantages (per site Reference)

## 5. Restriction Banners (§7.2)

- Only apply to **faceup** cards (not facedown)
- **Tree** (site icon): can only be played, moved, or swapped to a site (not advisers)
- **Person** (adviser icon): can only be played, moved, or swapped to advisers (not sites)
- **Chain** (locked): once placed, cannot be discarded, moved, or swapped — except in Chronicle (§8)

## 6. Ruling Sites (§9.5 Glossary: "Rule")

- You rule a site if you have **any** of your warbands on it
- You rule any cards at sites you rule
- You rule cards in your advisers
- Bandits rule sites with no player warbands (if bandits are present via site defense)
- A site can only hold warbands of one color at a time — so generally one ruler at a time
- Imperial players: Chancellor and Citizens both rule every site with any purple warbands
