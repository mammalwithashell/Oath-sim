# Oath Rules Summary: Core Rules & Turn Structure

Reference: Law of Oath (sections cited as §X.Y), Playbook for examples.

## 1. The Map

- 3 regions: **Cradle** (2 sites), **Provinces** (3 sites), **Hinterland** (3 sites) = 8 total
- Each region has a facedown **discard pile** for denizen/vision cards
- 6 **favor banks** (one per suit: Discord, Arcane, Order, Hearth, Beast, Nomad)
  - 3 favor tokens each (4 with 5-6 players)
- **World Deck**: denizens + visions facedown; sits on the map
- **Visions Drawn track**: tracks how many visions drawn from world deck (0-5)
- **Round track**: tracks current round (1-8)

## 2. Sites (§2.8)

- **Card capacity**: shown in top-right corner (how many denizen/edifice cards fit)
- **Reveal prompt**: top-left corner shows favor/secrets/relics placed when site is flipped faceup
- **Defense die and bandit**: top-right silhouette = 1 defense die + 1 bandit when targeted in campaign
- **Relic recover cost**: bottom-right corner
- **Site power**: bottom-left corner (described on Site Reference aid sheet)
- You **rule** a site if you have any warbands on it. Only one color of warbands per site.

## 3. Setup (Standard / Chronicle)

1. Place map, deal chronicled site cards + attached denizens/edifices/relics to slots
2. Round marker on space 1, Visions Drawn marker on space 0
3. Shared bank: 10 attack dice, 6 defense dice, 1 end die, 36 favor, 20 secrets
4. People's Favor: 1 favor on it. Darkest Secret: 1 secret on it
5. 3 favor in each of 6 favor banks
6. Chancellor: takes board, 24 warbands, pawn, supply marker, Imperial Reliquary, Grand Scepter
7. Each other player: Exile or Citizen board (chronicled side), 14 warbands, pawn, supply marker
8. Chancellor: 2 favor + 1 secret on board. Places 3 warbands on board, 2 on top Cradle site, 1 on each faceup site with denizen/edifice
9. Each Exile/Citizen: 1 favor + 1 secret. Exiles place 3 own-color warbands. Citizens place 3 purple
10. Chancellor takes Oathkeeper title on Oathkeeper side
11. Chancellor takes banner matching the Oath (Devotion→Darkest Secret, People→People's Favor)
12. 3 cards from bottom of world deck → 1 in each discard pile
13. Each player draws 3 cards from bottom of world deck
14. Advance Visions Drawn by number of visions drawn during setup
15. Each player places pawn on a faceup site, chooses 1 card as facedown adviser, discards other 2

## 4. Turn Structure (§4)

Played over up to 8 rounds. Each round: Chancellor first, then clockwise. Each turn has 3 phases:

### 4.1 Wake Phase (§4.1)

Resolve in order:
1. **People's Favor**: if you hold it, resolve its Wake power (§7.3.1)
   - Place or return 1 favor to/from a favor bank with least favor (ties: your choice)
   - If Mob side: repeat once more
   - If People's Favor reaches 6 favor: flip to Mob side (if not already)
2. **Check for Win** (Exiles only): check Usurper Win (§3.1) and Visionary Win (§3.2)
3. **Flip to Usurper**: if you're an Exile holding the Oathkeeper title, flip it to Usurper side
4. **Use Site Power**: if your pawn is at Salt Flats, Mine, or Drowned City, take 1 favor or secret

### 4.2 Act Phase (§4.2)

- Take any number of **major actions** (§5) and **minor actions** (§6) in any order
- Must finish one action before starting the next
- May take the same action multiple times
- May take no actions at all
- May also use "Action:" card powers (§7.3.2) — these cost no supply but may cost favor/secrets
- **Supply**: each major action costs supply. Move supply marker right by the cost amount.

### 4.3 Rest Phase (§4.3)

Resolve in order:
1. **Return Favor**: move all favor tokens on denizens/edifices to their matching suit favor banks
2. **Return Secrets**: move all secret tokens on denizens/edifices/relics to your board. Flip facedown secrets on your board to faceup
3. **Refresh Supply**: move supply marker to the space showing the number of warbands in your personal bank (not on map or board). Citizens refresh to Chancellor's supply level
4. **Save Supply**: move supply marker 1 extra space left for each unspent supply this turn. Cannot go past leftmost space
5. **Use Rest Powers**: resolve any "Rest:" card powers (§7.3.4), once each, any order

## 5. Major Actions

### 5.1 Search (§5.1) — Cost: 2-4 Supply

1. **Spend Supply**: 2 Supply for discard pile. World deck cost = shown on Visions Drawn track (2 at 0 visions, up to 4 at 5 visions)
2. **Draw 3 Cards**: from world deck OR discard pile of your region (not both). If drawing from world deck and you draw a Vision, stop drawing immediately and advance Visions Drawn
3. **Discard Cards**: discard all but one. Cradle discards go to Provinces pile, Provinces→Hinterland, Hinterland→Cradle
4. **Play One Card**: play to your site (§5.1.4.I), your advisers (§5.1.4.II), or your Revealed Vision space — or discard it (Exiles only can discard)
   - Playing to site: card faceup, gain 1 favor from matching suit bank. Cannot play if site at capacity. Tree restriction = site only. Person restriction = adviser only
   - Playing to advisers: faceup or facedown, max 3 advisers. If 4th, must discard one first
   - Playing Visions: Exiles play faceup to Revealed Vision space (not an adviser). Chancellor/Citizens can only play Conspiracy or play Visions facedown as advisers

### 5.2 Muster (§5.2) — Cost: 1 Supply

1. Place 1 favor on a denizen/intact edifice at your site (must have no favor or secrets on it, not a relic or ruin)
2. Gain 2 warbands from your personal bank to your board. Citizens gain purple warbands

### 5.3 Trade (§5.3) — Cost: 1 Supply

Two options:
- **Trade for favor**: place 1 secret on empty denizen/edifice at your site. Gain 1 favor from matching bank + 1 additional favor per faceup adviser matching that card's suit
- **Trade for secrets**: place 2 favor on empty denizen/edifice at your site. Gain 1 secret from shared bank per faceup adviser matching that card's suit
- Cannot trade with relics or ruins (§2.9)

### 5.4 Recover (§5.4) — Cost: 1 Supply

1. Choose a facedown relic at your site, or one banner (People's Favor or Darkest Secret)
   - Darkest Secret: can only recover if no card at holder's site mismatches your advisers' suits, or recovering from yourself
   - Cannot recover Darkest Secret from another player if they have no faceup advisers
2. **Pay Cost**: relic cost shown on site. Banners cost: favor > current value (People's Favor) or secrets > current value (Darkest Secret)
3. **Take It**: place relic/banner faceup near your board
4. **Resolve Banner**: if took People's Favor, flip to Mob if needed, redistribute its favor. If took Darkest Secret, take 1 secret, give rest to previous holder

### 5.5 Campaign (§5.5) — Cost: 2 Supply

(See rules-combat-cards.md for full campaign rules)

### 5.6 Travel (§5.6) — Cost: 1-4 Supply

Cost depends on origin and destination regions:
- **Same region**: Cradle→Cradle = 1. Provinces→Provinces = 2. Hinterland→Hinterland = 3
- **Adjacent region**: Cradle↔Provinces = 2. Provinces↔Hinterland = 2
- **Cross-map**: Cradle→Hinterland = 4. Hinterland→Cradle = 4

Move pawn to destination. If site is facedown, flip it faceup and resolve its reveal prompt (place favor, secrets, relics from banks/deck as shown).

## 6. Minor Actions (§6) — Cost: 0 Supply

- **Play/Discard Facedown Adviser** (§6.1): flip faceup or discard. Same rules as Search play step
- **Use an Action Power** (§6.2): use "Action:" power on accessible card (§7.3.2)
- **Peek at a Relic** (§6.3): look at any facedown relic at your site
- **Peek at Imperial Relic** (§6.4): Grand Scepter holder can peek at Reliquary relics
- **Move Warbands** (§6.5): move any number (except last one) from your site to your board or vice versa if you rule the site. Imperial players can exchange warbands with Chancellor's permission
- **Offer Citizenship** (§6.6): Grand Scepter holder can offer citizenship to Exiles
- **Exile a Citizen** (§6.7): Grand Scepter holder can exile Citizens (costs 5 favor, modified by titles/banners held)
- **Self-Exile** (§6.8): Citizens can exile themselves (costs favor equal to secrets on their board + warbands on their cards/Grand Scepter holder)

## 7. Supply Rules

- Chancellor: supply track goes from 0 to 6+ based on warbands in personal bank
- Exiles: same supply track mechanics
- Citizens: refresh to match Chancellor's supply level
- Supply cannot exceed the leftmost (maximum) space
- Unspent supply carries over via Save Supply (§4.3.4): +1 extra space per unspent supply
- Each major action shifts supply marker right by its cost
