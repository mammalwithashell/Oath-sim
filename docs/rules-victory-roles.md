# Oath Rules Summary: Win Conditions, Roles & Chronicle

Reference: Law of Oath (sections cited as §X.Y).

## 1. The Four Oaths (§2.11)

Each game uses one Oath, which determines the **Oathkeeper goal**. The Oathkeeper title has two sides: Oathkeeper (held by whoever meets the goal) and Usurper (flipped to by Exiles who take the title).

| Oath | Oathkeeper Goal | Successor Goal (for Citizens) |
|------|----------------|-------------------------------|
| **Supremacy** | Rule the most sites | Hold more relics + banners than Chancellor and any other Citizen |
| **The People** | Hold the People's Favor | Hold the Darkest Secret |
| **Devotion** | Hold the Darkest Secret | Hold the People's Favor |
| **Protection** | Hold the most relics + banners (combined) | Hold the Grand Scepter (special: see §2.4) |

### Oathkeeper Title Mechanics

- The title is held by the player currently meeting the Oathkeeper goal (§2.10)
- **Taking the title**: whenever a player takes the Oathkeeper title, it flips to its Oathkeeper side (if not already)
- **Exile flip**: if an Exile holds the title at the start of their turn (Wake Phase §4.1.3), it flips to Usurper side
- **Tied goals**: if the holder is tied with another player, the holder keeps the title. If multiple non-holders are tied, the holder chooses who takes it
- **Supremacy special**: Chancellor always holds the Oathkeeper of Supremacy, but all Imperial players share its power
- The Oathkeeper adds 1 defense die in Campaign (§5.5.2). The Usurper adds 2 defense dice

## 2. Four Ways to Win

### 2.1 Usurper Win (§3.1) — Start of Turn

- **Who**: any Exile
- **When**: checked during Wake Phase (§4.1.2)
- **Condition**: you hold the Oathkeeper title on its **Usurper side**
- **How it works**: an Exile takes the Oathkeeper title → it's on Oathkeeper side → at start of their NEXT turn, it flips to Usurper → they win immediately
- This means an Exile must hold the title through a full round of other players' turns

### 2.2 Visionary Win (§3.2) — Start of Turn

- **Who**: any Exile (or Citizen with a revealed Vision, though Citizens normally can't reveal one)
- **When**: checked during Wake Phase (§4.1.2)
- **Condition**: you have a **revealed Vision** card faceup AND you meet that Vision's goal AND **at least 3 Visions have been drawn** from the world deck (Visions Drawn track ≥ 3)
- **Vision goals**:
  - **Vision of Conquest** (221): you rule the most sites (strict majority)
  - **Vision of Rebellion** (223): you hold the People's Favor
  - **Vision of Faith** (222): you hold the Darkest Secret
  - **Vision of Sanctuary** (224): you hold the most relics + banners (strict majority)
  - **Vision of Conspiracy** (225): you have the most secrets (strict majority)

### 2.3 Stable Regime Win (§3.3) — End of Round

- **When**: at the end of rounds 5, 6, 7, or 8 (after all players have taken turns)
- **Die roll**: roll the end die to determine if the game ends:
  - **Round 5**: game ends on a roll of 6 (1/6 chance)
  - **Round 6**: game ends on a roll of 5 or 6 (2/6 chance)
  - **Round 7**: game ends on a roll of 3, 4, 5, or 6 (4/6 chance)
  - **Round 8**: game always ends (no roll needed)
- **Who can win**: only if the Chancellor or a Citizen is the Oathkeeper
- **Successor check** (§3.3.1): if the Chancellor would win and a Citizen meets the Successor goal, that Citizen wins instead
  - Supremacy (Sites): Citizen holds more relics + banners than Chancellor AND any other Citizen
  - The People (People's Favor): Citizen holds the Darkest Secret
  - Protection (Relics + Banners): Citizen holds the People's Favor
  - Devotion (Darkest Secret): Citizen holds the Grand Scepter

### 2.4 War Exhaustion Win (§3.4) — End of Round 8

If the game reaches the end of round 8, determine winner by checking in order:
1. **Empire if Oathkeeper** (§3.4.1): if Chancellor/Citizen is Oathkeeper → Citizen wins if meeting Successor goal, else Chancellor wins
2. **Usurper** (§3.4.2): if any Exile is Usurper → that Exile wins
3. **Visionary** (§3.4.3): if any Exile has a revealed Vision meeting its goal → that Exile wins. Tiebreaker order: Conquest > Rebellion > Sanctuary > Faith
4. **Empire** (§3.4.4): Chancellor wins. Citizen wins instead if meeting Successor goal

## 3. Roles

### 3.1 Chancellor

- Rules the empire. Starts with Oathkeeper title, Grand Scepter, Imperial Reliquary with 4 relics
- Has 24 warbands (purple). All purple warbands on the map are Imperial
- Controls citizenship offers (§6.6) and exiling Citizens (§6.7)
- **Imperial players** (§6.6.3): Chancellor + all Citizens. They share purple warbands on the map. Every site with any purple warbands is ruled by all Imperial players
- Chancellor wins by default if the Oathkeeper title stays on their side through the end-game die roll

### 3.2 Exile

- Begins as a subjugated outsider. Has 14 warbands of their own color
- Two paths to victory:
  1. **Usurper**: take the Oathkeeper title and hold it through a full round
  2. **Visionary**: reveal a Vision card and fulfill its condition (with 3+ visions drawn)
- Can accept Citizenship offers to become a Citizen
- Can play Vision cards faceup to their Revealed Vision space

### 3.3 Citizen

- An Exile who accepted Citizenship (§6.6.2). Board flips to Citizen side
- **Becomes Imperial**: replaces all warbands with purple, shares Imperial ruling
- **Discards revealed Vision** (if any) — cannot win as Visionary
- **Wins via Successor goal**: beat the Chancellor on the Successor condition when the game ends by die roll
- **Supply**: refreshes to match Chancellor's supply level (§4.3.3)
- Can **self-exile** (§6.8) to flip back to Exile

### 3.4 Offering Citizenship (§6.6)

- Only the **Grand Scepter holder** can offer (minor action)
- Must offer exactly **1 relic from the Reliquary**
- Can also negotiate binding exchanges of favor, secrets, banners, non-Reliquary relics, warbands
- Can let the Exile peek at Reliquary relics first
- **Accepting** (§6.6.2): Exile flips board to Citizen, replaces all warbands with purple, discards revealed Vision, flips Oathkeeper to Oathkeeper side if they held it on Usurper, ends Act Phase, refreshes Supply
- The Grand Scepter holder and new Citizen exchange the promised items

### 3.5 Exiling a Citizen (§6.7)

- Grand Scepter holder pays **5 favor**, modified:
  - −1 if you are Oathkeeper or hold People's Favor (each)
  - +2 if target is Oathkeeper or holds People's Favor (each)
- Target Citizen: flips board to Exile, replaces warbands with own color, refreshes supply

### 3.6 Self-Exile (§6.8)

- Any Citizen (except Grand Scepter holder) can self-exile
- **Cost**: give favor equal to secrets on your board + warbands on your cards to the Grand Scepter holder. Also give the value of each denizen, relic, edifice card, plus the Grand Scepter's value in warbands
- Flip to Exile, replace warbands, refresh supply, end Act Phase

## 4. Chronicle — What Persists Between Games (§8)

After a game ends, the **winner** performs these steps:

### 4.1 Vow an Oath (§8.1)

- Vision winner: use the goal reference matching that Vision's Oath
- Any other win: choose any goal reference except the current one (or keep it if won multiple ways)

### 4.2 Offer Citizenship (§8.2)

- If you're an Exile, flip all Citizen boards to Exile, then offer Citizenship to any Exiles (except those who just flipped)
- Accepting: flip board to Citizen, replace warbands with your color at sites they rule

### 4.3 Clean Up Map and Build Edifices (§8.3)

- Chancellor/Citizen winner can build or repair 1 edifice at each site they rule with no intact edifice (replace a denizen with matching-suit edifice from Archive)
- Discard all sites except those with intact edifices
- Flip intact edifices at unruled sites to ruined side, discard denizens there
- Set aside ruins with their attached cards, restack sites from Cradle to Hinterland

### 4.4 Add 6 Cards to World Deck (§8.4)

Winner's most common adviser suit determines cards drawn from Archive:
- 3 cards of most common suit, 2 of next clockwise, 1 of next clockwise after that

### 4.5 Remove 6 Cards to Dispossessed (§8.5)

Shuffle discard piles + losing players' advisers → take 6 random cards to Dispossessed section of Archive

### 4.6 Clean Up Relics (§8.6)

- Grand Scepter to box. Return losing players' relics to relic deck, shuffle
- Each faceup site gets relics to match its "R" count. Winner's relics + Reliquary relics shuffled on top of relic deck

### 4.7 Save Map and Boards (§8.7)

- Stack sites with their cards, ordered bottom Hinterland to top Cradle
- Save player boards on current side (Exile or Citizen)

### 4.8 Rebuild World Deck (§8.8)

- Shuffle remaining denizen cards, then build the world deck:
  - Take 10 denizens facedown, shuffle in 2 Vision cards → pile of 12
  - Take 15 denizens facedown, shuffle in 3 Vision cards → pile of 18
  - Stack: pile of 12 on top of pile of 18, on top of remaining denizens

## 5. Technical Rules (§9)

### 5.1 Interpreting Rules (§9.1)

- Interpret literally. Do not infer unwritten rules
- The Law supersedes the Playbook and play aids (except Site Reference)
- Card powers (§7) can add new actions and trigger effects beyond the Law

### 5.2 Must, If Able, Cannot, Ignore (§9.2)

- "Must" overrides other rules
- "Cannot" overrides other rules, even "must"
- "Ignore" overrides "must" and "cannot" — but only if those rules are named
- "If able" = must fulfill if you can, but if you cannot, no penalty

### 5.3 Component Limits (§9.3)

- Game is component-limited except for secrets and dice
- If prompted to gain more than available, gain as many as possible

### 5.4 Public and Private Information (§9.4)

- **Private**: card fronts in discard piles, world deck card count, cards beyond the first in world deck, facedown card fronts, facedown adviser card fronts
- **Public**: everything else — card backs, discard pile counts, favor/secret counts, warbands
- A player holding a facedown adviser can peek at it and show others (or lie about it)

### 5.5 No Unprompted Losses (§9.5)

- Cannot voluntarily burn/sacrifice more than a cost or requirement prompts you to

### 5.6 No Unprompted Binding Promises (§9.6)

- Promises are not binding except for Citizenship offers (§6.6) and powers that say "binding" (§7.6.3)
